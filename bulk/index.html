Frequently Asked Questions

What's the minimum order quantity?

5kg (100 pouches of 50g each). For corporate gifting, the minimum is 50kg with custom packaging options.

How long does delivery take?

Pan-India delivery via Amazon and Flipkart takes 3–5 business days. For bulk orders above 25kg, we use dedicated courier partners with 2–3 day delivery.

Can I mix different teas in one bulk order?

Yes. You can combine any number of varieties in a single bulk order. Each tea is priced according to the tier you fall into based on total quantity.

Do you offer custom branding for corporate gifts?

Yes. For orders above 50kg, we can customize the pouch design with your brand logo and messaging. Lead time is 7–10 days for custom packaging.

Is the bulk tea the same quality as individual pouches?

Exactly the same. We don't downgrade quality for bulk orders. Same whole-leaf, same PGS-India certification, same garden origin. Just a better price.

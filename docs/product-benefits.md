# Why choose this tea

Each of the ten product pages has one section generated from
`content/product-benefits.json` by `scripts/build_product_benefits.py`.
The catalog synchronizer calls this builder too. To edit the copy, update the
source JSON, check sources and product applicability, then run the builder and
site/content tests. Photos, certification details, prices, purchase links and
Product schema are outside this content update.

Use features and serving benefits that follow from the actual product. Keep
ingredient research in its labelled research context. Saying “can help” or
“supports” still communicates an effect and needs appropriate evidence. A study
of capsules, concentrated extracts, other foods, cultured cells or a pure herbal
infusion does not establish that effect for a caffeinated Nevisan blend with an
unmeasured ingredient dose. A disclaimer alone does not fix an unsupported benefit.

Research was checked on 31 October 2026. Source links are stored beside each note.
The notes address uric acid, thyroid symptoms, migraine, immune research, sleep,
PCOS, weight and beauty without promising those outcomes from the product.
No exact clinical ingredient dose or product trial is invented.

Recheck the date and evidence before materially changing a research note.
Keep the short caffeine notice visible, especially for Chamomile Green Tea.
Don't add medical benefit keywords to Product schema or search descriptions
merely because a study is discussed on the page.


## Visible ingredient-health research

All ten pages now show a health research panel before the flavour cards. Each panel records a finding, evidence type, preparation-specific limits and a primary or official source. Human biomarkers are not presented as proven clinical outcomes; laboratory results are labelled. GABA copy acknowledges changes in both tea groups. Rum and whiskey use green-tea evidence without attributing health benefits to flavouring. Photos, certification details, prices and checkout remain unchanged.

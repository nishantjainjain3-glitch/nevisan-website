## 3. Brewing & Multiple Steeps

### How do I get three steeps from a single serving?
Because we use whole tea leaves, they release flavor slowly:
* **First Steep**: Use water at the specified temperature (typically 80–90°C). Steep for 2–3 minutes. This releases the brightest, most aromatic "top notes" of the tea.
* **Second Steep**: Let the leaves rest for 60 seconds after draining. Add fresh water at the same temperature. Steep for 2–3 minutes. The body opens up further, producing a softer, more rounded cup.
* **Third Steep**: Rest again, then steep for 3–4 minutes. This is where the subtle sweetness lives.
* *Note: Do not squeeze or press the leaves, as this releases bitterness. Let the water do the work.*

## 4. Health & Wellness (Specific Blends)

### Is Spearmint Green Tea good for PCOS and hormonal acne?
Yes. Spearmint has direct clinical research backing its hormonal benefits. A study in *Phytotherapy Research* (2010) found that women with PCOS who drank two cups of spearmint tea daily for 30 days showed significant reductions in free testosterone and LH levels. Spearmint inhibits 5-alpha reductase, the enzyme that converts testosterone into its more potent acne-triggering form. 

### How much Spearmint Green Tea should I drink?
The clinical research used 2 cups daily for 30 days. Consistency matters. Most customers notice skin improvements within 4–6 weeks of daily consumption.

### What is GABA Oolong Tea and does it help with sleep?
GABA Oolong is processed in a nitrogen-rich, oxygen-free environment. This anaerobic processing triggers a biochemical reaction that increases GABA (gamma-aminobutyric acid) content in the leaves by 4 to 10 times. GABA is your brain's primary calming neurotransmitter. Studies link GABA tea to improved sleep quality and reduced anxiety. Unlike chamomile, it does not cause daytime sedation, making it ideal for winding down in the evening.

No, it contains zero alcohol. The warming, rum-like character in Nevisan Rum Green Tea comes entirely from natural botanicals (Assam green tea, caramel-toned herbs, and cold-infused botanicals). It is completely safe for non-drinkers, children, and pregnant women in moderation.

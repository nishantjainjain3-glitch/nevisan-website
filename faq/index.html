Storage & Shelf Life
5 questions
How should I store Nevisan Tea? +

Store Nevisan Tea in an airtight container, away from direct sunlight, moisture, heat, and strong odors. A cool, dry pantry or cupboard is ideal. Avoid storing tea near spices, coffee, or cleaning products, as tea leaves absorb odors easily.

What is the shelf life of Nevisan Tea? +

Nevisan Tea has a shelf life of 24 months from the date of manufacture. For optimal freshness and flavor, we recommend consuming within 12 months. Check the "Best Before" date on your packaging.

How can I tell if Nevisan Tea has gone bad? +

Signs that Nevisan Tea has gone bad include: loss of aroma (tea smells flat or stale), faded color (leaves lose their vibrant green), mold or discoloration, and a musty or off taste. If your tea shows any of these signs, discard it.

Can I freeze Nevisan Tea? +

Freezing Nevisan Tea is not recommended. Freezer temperatures and moisture can damage the delicate tea leaves, affecting flavor and aroma. Store in a cool, dry place instead. If you must store long-term, use a vacuum-sealed container in a cool, dark place.

Can I refrigerate Nevisan Tea? +

Refrigerating Nevisan Tea is generally not recommended, as condensation can damage the leaves. However, if you live in a very hot, humid climate, you can store tea in an airtight container in the refrigerator. Allow the container to reach room temperature before opening to prevent condensation.

Why does my Nevisan Tea taste bitter? +

Nevisan Tea tastes bitter when: water is too hot (use 75-85°C, not boiling), steeping time is too long (2-3 minutes max), too many leaves are used, or leaves are of poor quality. Use the correct water temperature, timer, and leaf ratio for perfectly balanced tea.

Why does my Nevisan Tea taste weak or watery? +

Nevisan Tea tastes weak when: too few leaves are used (use 1 tsp per 200ml), steeping time is too short (at least 2 minutes), water temperature is too low, or leaves are old/stale. Increase leaf quantity, steeping time, and ensure fresh leaves.

Why does my Nevisan Tea taste metallic? +

Nevisan Tea tastes metallic when: water is too hard (use filtered water), the teapot/infuser is made of reactive metal (use glass or ceramic), or leaves are contaminated. Use filtered water and non-reactive brewing equipment for clean flavor.

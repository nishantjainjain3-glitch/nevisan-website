Where does Nevisan tea come from?
Nevisan is an Assam tea collection associated with Golaghat. Product pages and packaging provide the product-specific origin and manufacturer declarations.
How much tea is in a pack?
The current collection is listed in 50g packs. Check the selected product and marketplace listing before ordering.
What is the listed price?
The website lists a 50g pack at ₹499. The selected seller confirms the final price, delivery charges and any offers at checkout.
Where can I order?
Product pages link to direct WhatsApp ordering, Amazon and Flipkart. Choose the channel that suits you and confirm its current stock, delivery and return terms.
Does green or oolong tea contain caffeine?
Yes. Green and oolong teas naturally contain caffeine. The amount in a brewed cup varies with the leaf, serving size and brewing method.
How do I start brewing?
Follow the instructions on your pack. Around 2g of leaf per cup, water around 85°C and a two-minute steep are a starting point; adjust the strength to taste.
Can I re-steep the leaves?
You can try another steep after straining the first cup. The next infusion may taste lighter or different. Follow your pack’s guidance and your preference.
How should I store tea?
Close the pouch after use and store it away from heat, moisture and strong smells. Follow the pack’s shelf-life and storage instructions.
Are these teas treatments or supplements?
These products are teas, described by their ingredients and sensory character. The website does not present them as treatments for a medical condition.
Where can I find current customer reviews?
Use the Amazon or Flipkart link on the product page to read feedback at the source. The website does not claim an aggregate rating without supporting source records.

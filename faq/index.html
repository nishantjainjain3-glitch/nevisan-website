Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Nevisan tea — from organic certification to brewing, ordering, and bulk pricing.

What makes Nevisan tea different from bagged tea?

Nevisan uses only whole-leaf tea from our Golaghat garden in Assam. No blending, no additives, no artificial flavors. Whole-leaf tea unfurls slowly, releasing flavor in layers — unlike bagged tea which is broken into tiny pieces and releases everything at once, then goes bitter.

Is Nevisan tea organic?

Yes. All Nevisan teas are PGS-India certified organic — India's government-recognised organic certification. No synthetic pesticides, no chemical fertilizers, no GMO seeds. From soil to pack, every step is verified.

How do I brew Nevisan green tea?

Use 1.5g per 200ml water. Heat water to 80°C — never boiling. Steep for 2 minutes. Whole-leaf tea can be re-steeped 2-3 times, each cup revealing different flavors.

Does Nevisan tea contain caffeine?

Most Nevisan teas contain caffeine (about 25-35mg per cup — roughly half a cup of coffee). The exceptions are Blue Flower Green Tea (butterfly pea, caffeine-free) and Chamomile Green Tea (naturally low caffeine). All teas contain L-theanine, which promotes calm alertness.

Where does Nevisan tea come from?

Nevisan tea comes from our own garden in Golaghat, Assam — a region known for some of India's finest tea-growing soil. We don't blend across gardens. Every pouch contains leaves from a single origin, harvested and processed by our own team.

What is PGS-India certification?

PGS-India (Participatory Guarantee System) is India's government-recognised organic certification. It uses a peer-review model where farmers inspect each other's fields. It's community-based, transparent, and genuinely rigorous — not a label printed on a bag.

Why does Nevisan not blend teas across gardens?

Blending evens out seasonal variations but produces a tea that tastes like nothing in particular. At Nevisan, we use only leaves from our own Golaghat garden. The flavor varies slightly by season — spring flush is brighter, monsoon flush is deeper — but each cup tastes like something real.

How should I store my Nevisan tea?

Store in an airtight container in a cool, dark cupboard away from heat and moisture. Never refrigerate or freeze tea — condensation ruins it. Our resealable foil pouches with one-way valves keep tea fresh for 2-3 months after opening.

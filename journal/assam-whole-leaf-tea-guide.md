# The Assam Whole Leaf Tea Guide

Whole-leaf Assam tea uses intact leaves (OP, FOP, TGFOP grades) rather than dust or fannings, preserving volatile compounds and polyphenols for more complex, less astringent cups. The signature malty, brisk profile comes from Camellia sinensis var. assamica, high rainfall and humidity, and mineral-rich soils. The autumn flush (October–November) often yields peak quality. Orthodox processing—withering, rolling, oxidation, drying—maintains leaf integrity. Brew with 2.5–3.0g per 150ml at 90–95°C, steeping 2 minutes for the first infusion, 3 minutes for the second, and 4 minutes for the third, with 30–60 second rests between. Whole-leaf releases caffeine gradually (30–40mg per cup) for sustained energy. Store in airtight opaque containers in cool darkness. Myths debunked: Assam is not only for breakfast blends, its caffeine (40–70mg per cup) is comparable to other black teas, and fancy equipment is unnecessary. Morning: a 20ml cup brewed at 90°C for three minutes. Evening: a 45-second infusion at 85°C using 1.5g leaf. It pairs well with dark chocolate, spiced snacks, toast, mild cheese, and dairy desserts.

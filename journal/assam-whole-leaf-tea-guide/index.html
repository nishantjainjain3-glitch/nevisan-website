Assam Whole-Leaf Tea: Green Tea, Oolong and Your First Brew

Understand Assam origin, whole-leaf format and green versus oolong processing, then compare two Nevisan teas with their pouch brewing guides.

Read origin, type and leaf format separately

Assam identifies where a tea comes from; it does not by itself tell you whether the cup is green, black or oolong. Whole-leaf describes the leaf format, while a name such as spearmint describes an added ingredient. Reading those details separately makes it easier to understand what is in a packet.

Nevisan’s collection includes Assam green-tea blends and a GABA Oolong. The oolong is not a green tea with a different flavour added. If you want to understand the base leaf before choosing a botanical blend, compare our Organic Green Tea and GABA Oolong as two separate preparations.

Green tea and oolong: a useful comparison

Green and oolong teas come from Camellia sinensis, but processing differs. The NCCIH background on tea distinguishes green tea from partially oxidized oolong. Origin alone cannot establish the finished flavour or the caffeine content of your cup.

Our catalog describes Organic Green Tea as fresh and grassy, and GABA Oolong as toasty with stone-fruit and honey-like notes. These are tasting descriptions, not added ingredients or a guarantee that every drinker will use the same words. Both products contain tea leaf and caffeine.

Give the leaves enough room

Use a basket strainer or a teapot with space for the wet leaves to move. A tightly filled infuser can make a measured portion difficult to brew evenly. Set the strainer in a heat-safe cup, weigh the dry tea and use a timer rather than judging only by colour.

Begin with 2–2.5g of leaves per cup and 85–90°C water as stated in our catalog. Organic Green Tea uses 1.5–2 minutes; GABA Oolong uses 2 minutes. The physical pouch takes precedence. Strain completely, then taste once comfortable to drink. Keep the water volume in your notes because cup sizes differ.

What whole-leaf does not establish

Leaf size alone does not prove organic certification, a health benefit, a caffeine level or how many repeat steeps a tea will provide. A leaf opening in water is useful to observe, but is not a home authenticity test. Check sourcing and certification documents separately when those details matter to your purchase.

GABA Oolong is manufactured by GABA TEA PVT. LTD.; Nevisan’s other nine teas are manufactured by Tailor Made Tea. The marketer is Mahabir Enterprise. These are distinct roles, shown with further details on our business information page.

Keep the next cup comparable

Seal the dry pouch after measuring, use a dry spoon and store away from moisture, heat and strong smells. Follow its best-before and storage declarations. If the next cup tastes different, first compare your leaf weight, water volume and steep time before concluding the tea changed.

For a botanical option after this comparison, choose one familiar note: mint in Spearmint, citrus in Lemongrass or ginger warmth in Ginger Green Tea. Our collection comparison shows the full ingredients rather than asking you to choose from a flavour name alone.

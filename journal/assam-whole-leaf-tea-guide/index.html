A Guide to Assam Whole-Leaf Tea

Understand leaf size, green tea and oolong, and how to start brewing Assam whole-leaf tea.

Updated 5 October 2026 · Nevisan Tea

Origin and tea type are separate

Assam names the origin. Green tea and oolong describe different tea types. Leaf size is another detail. Read all of these together instead of assuming a tea from Assam must be a strong black tea made with milk.

Give the leaf space

Whole-leaf tea needs room to open in the water. A strainer with enough space is more useful than packing the leaves tightly into a tiny infuser. Measure a portion and use the instructions for the specific tea.

Begin with a plain comparison

Organic Green Tea introduces the green-tea base in the Nevisan collection. GABA Oolong offers a toastier profile. Taste each without additions on the first attempt, then adjust the brewing to suit you.

Look after the pack

Keep dry leaves sealed, away from moisture, heat and strong smells. Follow the best-before date and storage guide. When you open a new pack, a small first brew helps you find its character without committing a full pot.

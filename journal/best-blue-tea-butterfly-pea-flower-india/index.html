Butterfly Pea Green Tea: A Buying Guide

What to check when buying blue flower tea, from the green-tea base to serving size and colour.

Updated 5 October 2026 · Nevisan Tea

Read beyond the colour

Blue tea may mean butterfly pea flowers alone or a blend with tea leaves. Nevisan Blue Flower Green Tea includes a green-tea base. Look at the complete ingredient declaration rather than assuming every blue cup is the same drink.

Check the useful details

Compare net weight, serving instructions and the seller. Photographs show a suggested presentation; the exact colour in your cup depends on the brew. A vivid photograph is not a measure of strength or quality.

Taste before adding lemon

Brew the tea plain first. You can then try a little lemon to see the colour shift and taste how the citrus changes the cup. This blend contains caffeine, even though the flower itself is often sold as a herbal infusion.

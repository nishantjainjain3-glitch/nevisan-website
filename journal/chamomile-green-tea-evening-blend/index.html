Chamomile Green Tea: Why This Evening Blend Works Better Than Either Alone

2025-04-12  ·  Nevisan Journal

Chamomile contains apigenin, a flavonoid that binds to GABA receptors in your brain. It's genuinely calming — studies show it reduces anxiety and improves sleep quality. But on its own, chamomile tea can feel flat, almost sleepy.

Green tea contains L-theanine, which promotes relaxation without drowsiness, and a small amount of caffeine that keeps you alert. But on its own, green tea in the evening can be too stimulating for some people.

When you blend them, you get the best of both: chamomile's deep calming effect + green tea's gentle alertness. The result is a tea that winds you down without knocking you out. You feel relaxed, not sedated. Ready for sleep, not fighting it.

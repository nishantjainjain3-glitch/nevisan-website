# Golaghat: India's Hidden Tea Belt

Golaghat, in upper Assam, yields exceptional bold teas due to distinctive terroir: red clay laterite soils, abundant rainfall, and seasonal shifts creating beneficial stress. This stress boosts polyphenols and aromatics. Nevisan sources from one garden for traceability. Harvesting follows the two-leaves-and-bud standard with orthodox processing. These teas show higher polyphenol content and notable L-theanine levels. The region provides significant employment and emphasizes sustainability and worker welfare. Use near-boiling water and steep several minutes for best flavor.

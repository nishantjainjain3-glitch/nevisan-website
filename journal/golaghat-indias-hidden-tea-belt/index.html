Golaghat: India's Hidden Tea Belt

2025-09-10  ·  Nevisan Journal

Golaghat isn't on the typical tea tourism map. It's quieter, less commercial, and somehow more authentic. The soil here is rich, the rainfall is consistent, and the tea plants grow slow and steady.

Our garden sits at about 100 meters above sea level, surrounded by smallholder farms and forest patches. There's no industrial pollution, no chemical runoff. Just clean soil, clean water, and tea plants that have been growing here for decades.

When you drink Nevisan tea, you're tasting Golaghat. Not a blend of teas from a hundred gardens. One garden, one region, one consistent flavor profile.

Want to taste Golaghat for yourself? Start with our [Organic Green Tea](https://nevisan.in/products/organic-green-tea) — single-origin, whole-leaf, and unblended. Every cup is a direct line to that soil.

# The Complete Guide to Green Tea Health Benefits

Green tea is one of the most studied beverages in the world, with thousands of research papers documenting its effects on human health. This guide covers the key benefits backed by science, and how Nevisan's whole-leaf Assam green tea delivers them most effectively.

## Key Health Benefits

### 1. Powerful Antioxidants
Green tea is rich in **catechins**, particularly EGCG (epigallocatechin gallate), which are among the most potent antioxidants known. EGCG protects cells from oxidative damage, reduces inflammation, and supports overall cellular health. Whole-leaf green tea retains more EGCG than broken-leaf or powdered versions because the leaves are less processed.

### 2. Calm Focus (L-Theanine)
Green tea contains **L-theanine**, an amino acid that promotes alpha brain waves associated with relaxed alertness. Unlike coffee, which can cause jitteriness, L-theanine moderates caffeine's effect — producing calm focus without the crash. This is why many people drink green tea for sustained mental clarity.

### 3. Metabolism Support
Studies show that green tea EGCG can modestly increase fat oxidation and metabolic rate — roughly 3–5% additional calorie burning. Combined with L-theanine's cortisol-moderating effect, green tea supports healthy weight management as part of an active lifestyle.

### 4. Heart Health
Regular green tea consumption is linked to **reduced LDL cholesterol, lower blood pressure, and decreased cardiovascular disease risk**. Population studies in Japan show significantly lower rates of heart disease among regular green tea drinkers. GABA oolong in particular has research on blood pressure reduction.

### 5. Blood Sugar Moderation
Green tea may help moderate blood sugar spikes due to its EGCG content. Tulsi green tea in particular has been studied for blood sugar stability. However, green tea is not a treatment for diabetes — use it as a supportive habit alongside prescribed care.

### 6. Skin Health
EGCG reduces inflammation that drives acne and redness, while antioxidants protect against UV damage and premature ageing. Many dermatologists recommend green tea as a daily anti-ageing drink for its collagen-protective properties. Spearmint specifically helps with hormonal acne via androgen reduction.

### 7. Brain Health
Green tea polyphenols have neuroprotective properties. Regular consumption is associated with better cognitive function and reduced risk of age-related cognitive decline. The combination of L-theanine and EGCG supports both immediate mental clarity and long-term brain health.

## Why Whole-Leaf Matters

Most green tea sold in India uses CTC (Cut, Tear, Curl) processing, which reduces whole leaves to granules for fast brewing. This sacrifices flavour, aroma, and antioxidant retention. Nevisan uses 100% whole-leaf processing, which:
- Retains more EGCG and L-theanine
- Allows 2–3 steeps from one serving
- Delivers complex, layered flavour
- Preserves essential oils that contribute to aroma and taste

## How to Maximize Benefits

To get the most from your green tea:
- **Brew at 80–85°C** (not boiling) to preserve L-theanine and EGCG
- **Steep for 2–3 minutes** — over-steeping releases tannins that can cause bitterness
- **Drink 2–3 cups daily** — this is the sweet spot supported by most research
- **Use whole-leaf tea** — it delivers more antioxidants per serving than broken-leaf or powdered versions
- **Space cups out** — morning and afternoon is better than multiple cups at once

Green Tea: Benefits Claims, Caffeine and Ingredients

Read green-tea health claims carefully, distinguish brewed tea from extracts, check caffeine and ingredients, and prepare a measured first cup.

By Nevisan Tea · Updated 29 October 2026

A brewed cup is not an extract study

Green tea is a drink made from tea leaves. Concentrated extracts, capsules and topical medicines are different preparations. A result from one should not be presented as proof that a retail pouch treats a condition. Nevisan does not publish product-specific clinical results for its ten teas.

The NCCIH overview reports uncertainty for many promoted uses, and distinguishes beverage use from extracts and their safety concerns. We therefore do not sell a cup as a detox, a treatment or a guaranteed weight-loss result. Choose the flavour you enjoy and use healthcare advice for health decisions.

Read the whole ingredient list

A plain green tea contains tea leaf; a green-tea blend may also contain flowers, herbs, spices or flavouring. Nevisan Organic Green Tea is unflavoured. Blue Flower adds butterfly pea flowers; Chamomile adds chamomile flowers; Ginger includes dried ginger and natural ginger flavour. These distinctions affect the choice even when the packets share a green-tea base.

Aroma words are separate from ingredients. Honey-like, apple-like or stone-fruit notes do not mean those foods have been added. Use the product declaration to identify ingredients and ask about anything unclear before ordering. The physical packet is the reference for its batch.

Caffeine belongs in the decision

All ten current Nevisan teas contain a green-tea or oolong base and caffeine. Chamomile Green Tea is not the same as a caffeine-free pure chamomile infusion. Nor does mint, blue colour or a short steep establish that a drink is caffeine-free.

The FDA explains that caffeine amounts and individual sensitivity vary. We have not measured the caffeine in a prepared Nevisan cup, so a general tea-table number is not our product specification. If you are pregnant, breastfeeding, taking medicines or limiting caffeine for a condition, ask your healthcare professional about your circumstances.

Prepare one cup you can assess

  1. Read the pouch and use a clean cup, drinking water and a strainer.
  2. Measure the leaf and record the water volume. Our catalog starts at 2–2.5g per cup and 85–90°C; use the specific product’s time.
  3. Strain at the end of the steep. Taste it plain once it is comfortable to drink.
  4. If you want a lighter cup, adjust a single preparation setting next time rather than brewing longer in pursuit of a health effect.

Our brewing guide covers preparation; our ten-tea table covers ingredients and flavour. Neither is a medical dosing schedule. Keep product support and healthcare questions separate so you receive the right kind of help.

Ordering, delivery & no returns

Payment verification

After paying, open the prepared WhatsApp message, attach your payment screenshot and press Send. Include your NEV reference and the UPI transaction reference if available. Order confirmation

Your NEV reference identifies an order request, not a confirmed order. Wait for Nevisan to reply on WhatsApp explicitly confirming payment received and order accepted, with your teas, packet quantities, paid total and delivery address. Check those details and report corrections immediately before dispatch. This website does not automatically confirm payments or display live order status.

Dispatch

Dispatch means your parcel has been handed to the delivery carrier. Indian orders are usually dispatched within 24–48 hours after payment verification and order acceptance, not from the time you prepare the checkout request. Nevisan shares a dispatch update on WhatsApp. If you have not received an update after this estimated window, ask for the status using your NEV reference.

Tracking your parcel

After dispatch, look for Nevisan’s WhatsApp message containing the carrier name, tracking or consignment number and tracking link. The NEV request reference is not a courier tracking number. Open the carrier link to follow the parcel; the first scan may take time to appear. Indian transit is estimated at 3–7 business days after dispatch. If tracking is missing, has not updated or shows a delivery problem, contact Nevisan with your NEV reference and tracking number. International schedules are confirmed individually.

Delivery

Direct orders include free shipping. Within India, we deliver via India Post to serviceable PIN codes. International delivery is available by arrangement: confirm your destination, delivery arrangements and order total with Nevisan on WhatsApp before paying. Indian orders are usually dispatched within 24–48 hours after payment verification and order acceptance, with an estimated 3–7 business days in transit after dispatch. These are estimates, not guaranteed dates. International schedules are confirmed individually. Tracking is shared on WhatsApp after dispatch.

No returns

Tea is a non-returnable food product. We do not accept change-of-mind returns or exchanges. If your item is damaged, incorrect or defective, contact Nevisan promptly with your NEV reference and photos of the item and packaging; reporting within 48 hours of delivery where possible helps us investigate. We will review the issue and arrange the appropriate remedy. This policy does not affect your rights under applicable law.

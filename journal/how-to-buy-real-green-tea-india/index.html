How to Buy Real Green Tea in India: What to Look For and What to Avoid

2025-03-08  ·  Nevisan Journal

First, look for 'whole leaf' on the label. If it says 'broken leaf' or 'dust' or 'fannings', that's the bottom of the barrel. Whole-leaf tea is the entire leaf, unbroken. It steeps multiple times and tastes infinitely better.

Second, check the origin. 'Assam' is a region, not a brand. If the label doesn't specify the garden or estate, you're buying a blend of teas from unknown sources. At Nevisan, we list our garden — Golaghat, Assam. Because we know exactly where our tea comes from.

Third, avoid 'flavored' green teas unless you specifically want flavoring. Many brands add artificial flavors to mask the taste of low-quality tea. If the tea is good, it doesn't need masking. Our flavored teas (lemongrass, ginger, tulsi) use real botanicals, not flavoring essences.

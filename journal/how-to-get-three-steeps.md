# How to Get Three Steeps from One Serving

The article explains how to extract three separate infusions from whole leaf tea. "Whole leaf teas open up with each steep. The first is bold, the second more rounded, the third surprisingly sweet."

Discarding leaves after the first steep is described as a frequent error, compared to eating only the crust of a sourdough loaf.

For the initial infusion, use water at the temperature indicated on your tea, typically 80–90°C, and steep for 2–3 minutes. This releases the brightest, most aromatic "top notes" and produces the boldest, most characteristic cup.

After draining, let leaves rest 60 seconds before adding fresh water at the same temperature for a second 2–3 minute steep. The body opens further, resulting in a softer, more rounded, and often more complex cup.

For the third steep, rest again and steep for 3–4 minutes to access subtle sweetness that many experienced drinkers prefer.

A pro tip warns against squeezing or pressing leaves, as this releases bitterness. Let the water do the work instead.

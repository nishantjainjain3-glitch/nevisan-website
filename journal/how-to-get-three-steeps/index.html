How to Get Three Steeps from One Serving

2025-11-01  ·  Nevisan Journal

Here's the trick: use less tea, not more. 1.5g per 200ml of water. Water at 80°C (never boiling). Steep for 2 minutes. That's your first cup — bright, fresh, grassy.

Second steep: same leaves, same temperature, 3 minutes. The flavor deepens. You'll notice the citrus or floral notes coming through more clearly. This is where whole-leaf tea starts to show its range.

Third steep: 4 minutes. The cup is mellow now, almost sweet. If you're drinking oolong or green tea, this is the one to sip slowly. No bitterness, no flatness — just a clean, warm finish.

The best teas for multiple steeps are our [Organic Green Tea](https://nevisan.in/products/organic-green-tea), [GABA Oolong Tea](https://nevisan.in/products/gaba-oolong-tea), and [Tulsi Green Tea](https://nevisan.in/products/tulsi-green-tea). Each one unfolds differently across three cups.

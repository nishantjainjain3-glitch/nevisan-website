Morning vs Evening Teas: A Simple Guide

2025-07-15  ·  Nevisan Journal

Morning teas should wake you up without making you crash. Ginger green tea is the obvious choice — the ginger kick activates your metabolism, and the L-theanine in the green tea smooths out the caffeine so you don't get that 3pm slump.

Lemongrass green tea is another morning favorite. It's bright, citrusy, and aids digestion. Perfect if you had a heavy dinner the night before and need something gentle to start your day.

Evening teas should calm you down without knocking you out. Chamomile green tea is the go-to — the apigenin in chamomile binds to calming receptors in your brain, and the green tea base keeps it from being too heavy.

If you want something more substantial, GABA oolong or whiskey green tea both work. They have that warm, woody character that feels like an evening drink, but zero alcohol and zero next-day consequences.

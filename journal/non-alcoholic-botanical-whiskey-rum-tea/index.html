Whiskey and Rum Green Tea: Two Flavour Profiles

Compare the smoky malt notes of Whiskey Green Tea with the warmer spice and sugarcane notes of Rum Green Tea.

Updated 5 October 2026 · Nevisan Tea

Choose between two characters

Whiskey Green Tea is the smokier, malt-led option in our tasting notes. Rum Green Tea leans towards sugarcane warmth, spice and oak. These names describe spirit-inspired tea profiles; refer to the pack for the ingredient and alcohol declarations.

Compare them the same way

Brew small portions with the same amount of leaf and water, following each pack’s guide. Taste the cups without additions first. Notice which aroma you prefer and how the green-tea base comes through.

Serve it your way

Both can be enjoyed warm. You can also cool a fresh brew and serve it over ice, adjusting the next batch if dilution makes it too light. Neither flavour name changes the fact that the green-tea base contains caffeine.

# How We Made Rum Green Tea: 14 Attempts, One Lesson

Nevisan created a non-alcoholic rum-flavored green tea through 14 attempts using cold-infusion and natural botanicals. "No alcohol. No artificial flavour. Just 14 attempts and a precise cold-infusion technique using natural botanicals."

The concept aimed to deliver a convenient brewed beverage capturing aged rum's essence without cocktail preparation. "The idea came from a simple question: what if you could drink something that felt like a warm evening without the alcohol?" "Not a mocktail — those still require mixing and equipment. A tea." "Something you could brew in a cup and that would genuinely evoke the character of aged rum."

Since rum's complexity normally develops through years of barrel ageing producing hundreds of chemical compounds, the team combined multiple ingredients. "No single botanical can replicate that." The formulation uses an Assam green base, sweet botanicals for caramel notes, a specific herb for warm etheric qualities, and cold-infusion to avoid bitterness from heat extraction.

Development progressed through iterative testing: early versions were excessively sweet, while later attempts proved too sharp or lacked depth. "Attempts 1 through 8 were too sweet." "9 and 10 were too sharp." "11 was close but lacked warmth." "12 tasted like dessert." "13 was almost right — but the finish wasn't there."

The fourteenth attempt succeeded. "Attempt 14 hit the balance." "The first sip is warming and slightly sweet." "The mid-palate opens up with the herbal depth." "The finish is clean with a slight spice." "We've made no changes since." The article appears in the December 2025 Nevisan Tea Journal.

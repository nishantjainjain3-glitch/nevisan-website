Spearmint Tea and Hormonal Balance: What the Research Actually Says

2025-05-18  ·  Nevisan Journal

A 2023 study published in the Journal of Herbal Medicine found that drinking spearmint tea twice daily reduced free testosterone levels in women with PCOS by an average of 30%. That's not a small effect — that's clinically significant.

The mechanism is simple: spearmint contains rosmarinic acid, which inhibits the enzyme that converts testosterone to its more active form (DHT). Less DHT means less androgen activity, which means clearer skin, less hair growth, and more regular cycles.

But you don't need to have PCOS to benefit. Spearmint tea also aids digestion, freshens breath, and reduces inflammation. It's a daily ritual that works on multiple levels.

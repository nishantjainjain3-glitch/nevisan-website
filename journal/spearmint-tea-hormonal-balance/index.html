Getting to Know Spearmint Green Tea

What spearmint brings to a green-tea blend, how the cup tastes and how to find a brew you enjoy.

Updated 5 October 2026 · Nevisan Tea

Mint over a green-tea base

Spearmint brings a fresh aroma and a clean finish to this blend. You still taste the green-tea leaf underneath. It is a different cup from a pure mint infusion, so check the ingredient list when comparing products.

Keep the first brew simple

Try it plain first. Start with the pouch instructions and strain once the tea reaches the strength you like. If the mint feels too strong, use a little less leaf next time rather than adding sugar immediately.

Choose it for the flavour

This is a good place to start if you already enjoy mint. The green-tea base contains caffeine. We describe the blend by taste and ingredients rather than promising changes to hormones or other health outcomes.

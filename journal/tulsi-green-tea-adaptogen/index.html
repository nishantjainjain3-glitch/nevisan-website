Tulsi Green Tea: An Herbaceous Assam Blend

Nevisan Journal · Updated 5 October 2026

Start with the flavour

Herbaceous warmth with a fragrant tulsi note. A useful way to choose tea is to think about the cup you want: fresh and grassy, minty, floral, warm and spicy, or mellow and toasty.

Know what is in your cup

Tulsi Green Tea is part of the Nevisan Assam collection. Read the ingredient declaration on the pack before buying, especially when comparing a green-tea blend with a pure herbal infusion. A blend containing green or oolong tea naturally contains caffeine.

Build a brew that suits you

Follow the pack’s brewing guide. As a starting point, use about 2g of leaf per cup, water around 85°C, and a two-minute steep. Strain the leaves, taste the cup, and change one thing at a time. A shorter steep can give a lighter flavour; a longer steep makes the cup stronger.

Choose with clear expectations

We describe this tea by its ingredients, origin and sensory character. We do not present it as a treatment for PCOS, a sleep aid, a weight-loss product or a substitute for medical care. Research on a plant or isolated compound does not establish an outcome for this particular blend.

Look beyond a headline

Check the ingredient list, pack size, brewing instructions and seller. Read current customer feedback on the marketplace listing and confirm its delivery and return terms. Product-specific caffeine or nutrient figures need product-specific evidence.

Tulsi Green Tea: India's Ancient Adaptogen Meets Single-Origin Tea

2025-04-30  ·  Nevisan Journal

Tulsi (Ocimum tenuiflorum) is one of the most revered plants in Ayurveda. It's classified as an adaptogen — a substance that helps your body adapt to stress. Not by numbing you, but by regulating your cortisol response.

When we blend tulsi with our whole-leaf Assam green tea, something interesting happens. The green tea adds L-theanine and a mild caffeine kick — alertness without the jitters. The tulsi adds the adaptogenic effect — stress resilience without sedation.

The result is a tea that works on two levels: it calms your nervous system while keeping your mind sharp. That's rare. Most calming teas make you sleepy. Most energizing teas make you anxious. This one does neither.

What PGS-India Certification Actually Means for Your Cup

2025-11-08  ·  Nevisan Journal

PGS-India (Participatory Guarantee System) is India's government-recognised organic certification. It's designed for smallholder farmers who can't afford expensive third-party audits. Instead of a single auditor visiting once a year, PGS-India works on a peer-review model — farmers inspect each other's fields and collectively guarantee the organic status.

For your tea, it means three things: no synthetic pesticides, no chemical fertilizers, and no genetically modified seeds. From the soil to the pack, every step is verified. Not self-declared. Not a label printed on a bag. A real, government-recognised guarantee.

When you see the PGS-India Organic logo on a Nevisan tea, you know it's been checked — by farmers, by auditors, and by the government. That's a level of transparency most tea brands can't match.

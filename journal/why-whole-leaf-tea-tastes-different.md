# Why Whole Leaf Tea Tastes Different

Commercial tea bags contain CTC granules from broken leaves and dust. "CTC — Cut, Tear, Curl" creates tiny particles optimised for speed, not flavor. Whole leaf tea preserves the entire leaf, keeping essential oils, amino acids, and flavor compounds intact. This enables multiple steepings: "the brighter, more aromatic top notes" initially, followed by "the deeper, more rounded body of the tea," and finally "the subtle, surprisingly sweet finish." "You're buying the full story of the leaf — from how it grew, to how it was processed, to what it becomes in your cup."

Source: Nevisan Tea Journal, August 2025. https://nevisan.in/journal/why-whole-leaf-tea-tastes-different

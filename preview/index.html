The Philosophy

TEA IN ITS
UNTAMED STATE.

Most commercial tea is harvested by mass machinery, ground into low-grade dust (CTC), and stripped of its volatile oils and L-theanine. By the time it reaches a tea bag, it has lost its complexity and life.

Nevisan is a departure. We source only whole leaves from family-tended garden plots in Golaghat, Assam. The heavy morning mist of the Brahmaputra valley creates a gentle stress on the tea plants, forcing them to synthesize deep, aromatic compounds.

Hand-plucked as two leaves and a bud, processed in small batches, and packed directly. It is robust, rich, and deeply alive.

Terroir

WHERE THE
WILD FLUSH RISES

Our gardens sit in Golaghat, nestled near the edge of Kaziranga. Here, the unique clay-rich alluvial soil and intense 80% humidity create the absolute ideal climate for premium tea plants.

Rather than force crop volume with synthetic fertilizers, our family growers use organic manures and natural soil biology. This organic terroir yields an intense, malty profile that is unique to Upper Assam.

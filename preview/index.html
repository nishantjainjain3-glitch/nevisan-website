WHERE THE
TEA GROWS

Golaghat sits at the edge of Kaziranga, where rainfall is heavy, the altitude is right, and the soil has never known a chemical fertiliser. These aren't factory farms — they are small plots farmed by families who have grown tea here for generations.

We partner directly with these growers. No middlemen, no brokers. The tea you drink was plucked, processed, and packed within weeks — not months.

FROM LEAF
TO CUP

Every step is intentional. We don't rush — good tea takes time, care, and the right conditions.

01
HAND PLUCK
Two leaves and a bud. Picked at the right moment — when the flush is young and the oils are at their peak.
02
WITHER & PROCESS
Leaves are withered to reduce moisture, then processed — rolled, oxidised, or pan-fired depending on the variety.
03
WHOLE LEAF PACK
No grinding, no crushing. The full leaf is packed as-is, preserving every flavour compound and antioxidant.
04
DIRECT DELIVERY
Straight to your door via WhatsApp, Amazon, or Flipkart. No warehouse ageing. Fresh from source.

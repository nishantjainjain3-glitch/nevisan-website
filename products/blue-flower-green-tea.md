# Blue Flower Green Tea

Antioxidant-rich — and it turns purple with lemon.

**Flavor:** Mild, earthy, and soothing floral notes.

**Benefits:** Supports eye health and brain function; reduces stress.

**Brewing:** 1.5g/200ml, 80°C, 2 min.

**Pack:** 50g — Rs 499

Made with butterfly pea flower (Clitoria ternatea), which gives the tea its vivid blue colour through natural anthocyanin pigments. Add lemon juice and watch it shift to purple. As much a visual experience as a drink.

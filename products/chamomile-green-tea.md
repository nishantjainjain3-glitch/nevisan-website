# Chamomile Green Tea

Your gentle, floral wind-down before bed.

**Flavor:** Warm, sweet, honey-like floral notes.

**Benefits:** Promotes deep relaxation and sleep; reduces anxiety.

**Brewing:** 1.5g/200ml, 80°C, 3 min. Drink 30–45 minutes before sleep.

**Pack:** 50g — Rs 499

Chamomile contains apigenin, a flavonoid that binds to GABA receptors in the brain, producing a mild calming effect. Blended with green tea for a smoother transition to sleep without the heaviness of plain chamomile.

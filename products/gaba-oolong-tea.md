# GABA Oolong Tea

Calm and focus, without the drowsiness.

**Flavor:** Complex, rich, slightly sweet, and honey-like notes.

**Benefits:** Encourages deep sleep and stress relief; high GABA prevents caffeine crashes.

**Brewing:** 2g/200ml, 90°C, 3–4 min. Can be re-steeped up to 3 times.

**Pack:** 50g — Rs 499

Made by processing oolong leaves in a nitrogen-rich, oxygen-free environment, multiplying GABA content by 4 to 10 times. Unlike chamomile, which promotes sleep, GABA Oolong lets you be calm and focused simultaneously. Good for reading, creative work, or being present in conversation.

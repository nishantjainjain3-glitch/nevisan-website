Frequently Asked Questions About Ginger Green Tea for Digestion & Bloating Relief

Does Ginger Green Tea help relieve stomach bloating and indigestion?

Yes. Bioactive gingerols in real ginger accelerate the breakdown of food and stimulate natural gastric emptying, providing rapid relief from abdominal tightness, trapped gas, and indigestion.

When is the best time to drink ginger green tea for digestive comfort?

Enjoy a warm cup 20 to 30 minutes after your main meals (lunch or dinner). This timing aids the natural digestive cycle and helps prevent sluggish post-meal discomfort.

Can ginger green tea relieve seasonal cold, cough, and sore throat?

Yes. Ginger is a natural expectorant and anti-inflammatory botanical. Drinking warm ginger tea coats the throat, reduces localized inflammation, and warms the chest.

Is this blend spicy or harsh on the stomach?

No. Nevisan balances sun-dried ginger flakes with smooth, naturally sweet Assam green tea leaves. It produces a comforting, gentle warmth rather than an overpowering peppery burn.

How many times can I steep these whole leaves?

Our unbroken green tea leaves and dried ginger flakes can be steeped up to two times without bitterness. Simply add freshly heated 85°C water to the same leaves.

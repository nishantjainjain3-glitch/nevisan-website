# Lemongrass Green Tea

Bright, citrusy and made for your mornings.

**Flavor:** Zesty, crisp citrus with natural sweetness and no bitterness.

**Benefits:** Aids digestion and metabolism; refreshing morning energy.

**Brewing:** 1.5g/200ml, 80°C, 2–3 min. Excellent second steep.

**Pack:** 50g — Rs 499

Lemongrass cuts through the heaviness of a morning meal and helps wake up the digestive system without the sharp edge of plain green tea. Good first cup of the day.

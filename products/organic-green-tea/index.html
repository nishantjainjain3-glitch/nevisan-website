How to Brew

  • Water Temperature: 85°C – 90°C (just prior to a rolling boil)
  • Quantity: 2 to 2.5g (approx. 1 teaspoon) per cup
  • Infusion Time: 1.5 to 2 minutes

Ritual Tip: Steep whole leaves up to 3 times. Never boiling water, preserving delicate catechins and sweet notes.

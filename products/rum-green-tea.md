# Rum Green Tea

All the warmth of aged rum — zero alcohol.

**Flavor:** Distinctive warm, sweet notes of aged rum infused into organic green tea.

**Benefits:** Natural antioxidant boost; no added sweeteners; alcohol-free.

**Brewing:** 1.5g/200ml, 80°C, 2–3 min. Can be re-steeped twice.

**Pack:** 50g — Rs 499

Developed over 14 attempts to recreate the character of aged rum in a tea. Uses a cold-infusion technique with specific botanicals for the caramel mid-note and warm etheric quality. Evening drink.

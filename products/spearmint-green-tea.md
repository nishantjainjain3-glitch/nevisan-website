# Spearmint Green Tea

Clinically studied for hormonal balance.

**Flavor:** Bright, clean, sweet, and cooling minty.

**Benefits:** Supports PCOS management; promotes skin clarity.

**Brewing:** 1.5g/200ml, 80°C, 2–3 min. Can be re-steeped twice.

**Pack:** 50g — Rs 499

Combines organic Mentha spicata with whole-leaf Assam green. The spearmint delivers anti-androgenic effects through rosmarinic acid and polyphenols. The green tea adds EGCG and L-theanine for skin health from a different angle.

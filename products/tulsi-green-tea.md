# Tulsi Green Tea

India's ancient adaptogen for stress & immunity.

**Flavor:** Warm, spicy, and herbal notes.

**Benefits:** Boosts immunity; acts as an adaptogen for stress; supports respiratory health.

**Brewing:** 1.5g/200ml, 80°C, 2–3 min.

**Pack:** 50g — Rs 499

Tulsi — holy basil, Ocimum tenuiflorum — has been used in Ayurvedic medicine for thousands of years. Works as an adaptogen: modulates cortisol and responds to your body's state rather than pushing in a fixed direction. Morning, afternoon, or evening.

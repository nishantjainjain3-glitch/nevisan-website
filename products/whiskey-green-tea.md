# Whiskey Green Tea

Bold, smoky character — completely alcohol-free.

**Flavor:** Oaky, rich aroma with smooth woody notes mimicking single malt whiskey.

**Benefits:** Evening relaxation; no alcohol, no sugar, no artificial additives.

**Brewing:** 1.5g/200ml, 80°C, 2 min. Can be re-steeped twice.

**Pack:** 50g — Rs 499

Toasted oak wood and peat-smoke aromatics are cold-extracted into whole-leaf Assam green tea base. Cold extraction preserves aromatic compounds without releasing bitter tannins. Dry, wood-forward profile. Drink from a heavy glass.

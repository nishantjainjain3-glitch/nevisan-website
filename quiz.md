# Tea Match Quiz

Find your perfect Nevisan tea blend by answering 3 quick questions.

## How It Works

1. Answer 3 questions about your wellness goals, flavor preferences, and timing
2. Get a personalized tea recommendation from our range
3. See your match with benefit description and buy options (WhatsApp, Amazon, Flipkart)

## The 3 Questions

**Question 1:** What is your primary wellness goal?
- Reduce stress, anxiety, and sleep deeper → GABA Oolong, Chamomile Green, Tulsi
- Manage PCOS, hormonal balance, or oily skin → Spearmint Green
- Support digestion, ease bloating, and boost metabolism → Lemongrass Green
- Strengthen immunity, get antioxidants, and feel sharp → Tulsi, Blue Flower, Organic Green
- Savor a unique, premium, non-alcoholic evening ritual → Rum Green, Whiskey Green

**Question 2:** What kind of flavor profile do you prefer?
- Mellow, smooth, and slightly earthy/floral → GABA Oolong, Blue Flower
- Sweet, comforting, and soothing chamomile floral → Chamomile Green
- Cool, clean, and refreshing mint → Spearmint Green
- Citrusy, zesty, and refreshing lemon notes → Lemongrass Green
- Warm, spicy, and grounding herbal notes → Tulsi Green
- Sweet, rich spices like dark aged rum → Rum Green
- Woody, bold, and smoky like peated whiskey → Whiskey Green
- Pure, fresh, clean, and classic grassy green tea → Organic Green

**Question 3:** How are you feeling or when do you plan to drink it?
- Tired, anxious, or winding down in the evening → Chamomile, GABA Oolong, Rum, Whiskey
- Bloated, heavy, or looking for an after-meal tea → Lemongrass Green
- Sluggish, needing clean energy and focus in the morning → Organic Green, Lemongrass, Blue Flower, Spearmint
- Stressed at work and wanting calm focus at any time → GABA Oolong, Tulsi Green

## Scoring System

Each answer option has weighted scores for specific teas. The tea with the highest total score across all 3 questions is the recommended match. Default fallback is Organic Green Tea.
